Our Mission

NeuroHope creates a pathway for young people to go from learning about neuroscience → meeting the people doing the research → conducting/engaging in research → educating others → creating community resources → advocating for change.

📚 Learn

Explore neuroscience education and understand neurological disorders

🔬 Research

Connect with researchers and engage in meaningful projects

🤝 Advocate

Build community and create resources for change

💬 Connect

Share stories and support others on their journeys
