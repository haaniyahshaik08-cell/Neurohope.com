Empowering Change Through Knowledge, Advocacy, and Hope

NeuroHope is a youth-led Initiative that strives to empower the next generation to turn talk into action and create meaningful conversations around brain health. We care deeply about advocacy around increasing awareness, education, and stigma-busting around neuro and neurodevelopmental conditions like Alzheimer's, autism and ADHD.

Join Our Initiative

Why We Matter

  • There's a gap between science and society — and we're bridging it. Over 60% of people worldwide lack access to brain health education (WHO).
  • Youth are left out of key health conversations — we're changing that. Young people make up 40% of the world, but rarely have a voice in healthcare advocacy.
  • Neuroscience is missing in schools — we're filling that gap. Only 11% of U.S. high schools offer neuroscience-related education (NIH).

What We Do

Educational Workshops

  • Lead interactive workshops on Alzheimer's, autism, ADHD
  • Simplify complex neuroscience topics for students

Fundraising & Crafting

  • Handcraft and sell bracelets and crochet items
  • Donate handmade gifts to caregivers
